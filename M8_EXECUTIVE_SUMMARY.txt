WHAT IS M8?
================================================================================

M8 adds artificial intelligence-powered merchant insights to the payment 
recovery platform. Merchants now receive daily AI-generated analyses and 
recommendations for:

  • Understanding why payments fail
  • Identifying abandoned cart patterns  
  • Optimizing recovery strategies
  • Recommending product bundles
  • Suggesting discount strategies
  • Improving inventory levels
  • Targeting high-value recovery opportunities

All recommendations include built-in safety guardrails to protect merchant 
interests and maintain business logic compliance.
